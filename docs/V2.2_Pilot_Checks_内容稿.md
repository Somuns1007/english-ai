## Pilot A — Set1 Conversation 1(职场访谈)

材料事实摘要(provenance 锚点):
- seg001 [M]: 节目 Business Matters;邀请 career development consultant Lucy Williams;议题 how to get ahead at work
- seg002 [W]: 错误=只和老板沟通;要扩展 network 到同事
- seg004 [W]: 不要低估同事;job title 不一定体现 influence
- seg005–006 [M→W]: M 提出"working hard creates a good impression, but this isn't always true, is it?" W 确认(That's right),应避免 take on too much work / constantly staying late 来显示 committed
- seg007–008 [M→W]: M 总结"开始把期望值抬太高,维持不住反而难看";W:quality time > quantity time;努力可由 results 证明
- seg010 [W]: 帮助需要的人,**但前提是**自己的工作不被耽误;帮助别人能赢得 respect and trust
- seg011–012 [M→W]: 不要表现得像 know-it-all,否则显得 arrogant;要 humble

### Check A1(speaker_relationship)

**Question:** What is the relationship between the two speakers?

- A) A boss and an employee discussing how to get promoted
- B) A program host and a career development consultant ✓
- C) Two coworkers complaining about their manager
- D) A job interviewer and a candidate in a job interview

**正确答案需要理解的关系:** 话轮功能(邀请-提问-解答的访谈结构)+ seg001 的显性身份介绍("we have invited career development consultant Lucy Williams")。

**干扰项迷惑性:**
- A:boss / promoted 都是原文真词(seg001 get promoted、seg002 bosses);把"谈论老板"偷换成"和老板说话",把节目场景偷换成上下级场景。
- C:coworkers 是原文真词(seg002/003);但对话中没有一个人抱怨自己的 manager——抱怨主体错位。
- D:M 确实在"提问",表面像面试;但 M 是替 "many of us" 发问(节目主持功能),且被访者是"被邀请的顾问",不是求职者。

**为什么不能靠关键词:** 四个选项全部由原文高频词(boss/promoted/coworker/interview 功能)构成;必须理解"谁在什么角色里说话",纯词匹配会命中全部四个选项。

**Provenance:** seg001(身份介绍)、seg002(bosses 词)、seg003(coworkers 词)。

### Check A2(change_tracking / attitude)

**Question:** The man mentions a common belief that working hard creates a good impression. What is the woman's response?

- A) She confirms it, adding that constantly staying late shows commitment
- B) She agrees the belief does not always hold, and advises putting in quality time rather than just a large quantity of time ✓
- C) She suggests setting high expectations at the beginning to impress others
- D) She recommends taking on more work so that the results can prove one's effort

**正确答案需要理解的关系:** "普遍信念(seg005)→ W 确认其不可靠(That's right, seg006)→ 修正方向(quality > quantity, seg008)"这一完整的"提出-否定-修正"链。

**干扰项迷惑性:**
- A:staying late / commitment 是原文真词,但 W 的态度恰好相反(should avoid)。测否定方向的跟踪。
- C:setting expectations 出现在 seg007,但那是 M 对"期望抬太高有坏处"的总结,且 W 从未建议抬高期望——内容张冠李戴 + 方向反转。
- D:results / effort 是原文真词(seg008),但 W 说的是"既有努力可由结果证明",不是"接更多活来挣表现";take on too much work 正是她警告要避免的(seg006)。测因果方向。

**为什么不能靠关键词:** 每个干扰项都嵌入了原文动词短语(staying late / expectations / results);区分点全在"肯定还是否定""谁说的""因还是果"。

**Provenance:** seg005、seg006、seg007、seg008。

### Check A3(final_position / scope)

**Question:** What is the woman's advice about helping colleagues with their projects?

- A) Help whenever someone asks, even if your own work falls behind
- B) Help those who need it, but only as long as your own work does not go unattended ✓
- C) Focus on your own responsibilities and decline such requests
- D) Help mainly in order to gain others' respect and trust

**正确答案需要理解的关系:** 建议成立的条件范围(scope):"only as long as your own work will not go unattended" 是建议的一部分,不是附加闲聊。

**干扰项迷惑性:**
- A:拿掉条件句,把有限建议绝对化——测学生是否把"but only as long as"从记忆中丢掉。
- C:过度矫正;W 明确支持帮助(Extend your assistance),并非拒绝。测肯定/否定大方向。
- D:respect and trust 是原文真词,但原文里它是帮助带来的**结果**("help them out to gain their respect and trust"),不是建议的**目的/条件**。测 cause/result 错位。

**为什么不能靠关键词:** help / own work / respect / trust 在四个选项中均出现;只有抓住条件从句的限定范围才能区分 A 与 B。

**Provenance:** seg010(唯一依据)、seg009(问题由来)。

## Pilot B — Set2 Conversation 1(同事闲聊:筹备周年纪念旅行)

材料事实摘要(provenance 锚点):
- seg002 [M]: 下周是 tenth wedding anniversary;答应 Jane 订 city break;**有** annual leave 可休;在订 Barcelona
- seg003 [W]: 喜欢 Barcelona;大教堂建了约 150 年仍在建
- seg004 [M]: 问题=住宿 wildly expensive or already fully booked;感到 frantic
- seg005 [W]: 提议=几晚 glamorous + 其余时间去海岸线上更便宜处,用低价对冲高价
- seg006 [M]: 接受("That might be a good plan");补充:城外有 all-inclusive 带泳池的地方;准备发询价
- seg007 [W]: 推荐大教堂登顶,但要走很陡的旋转楼梯
- seg008 [M]: **Jane** 恐高("Jane is terrified of heights"),所以不会去登顶

### Check B1(main_situation)

**Question:** What problem is the man facing?

- A) His request for annual leave has not been approved
- B) He almost forgot about the upcoming anniversary
- C) Accommodation in Barcelona is either too expensive or already fully booked ✓
- D) Jane is not willing to go to Barcelona

**正确答案需要理解的关系:** 主问题定位——seg004 是明确的 problem statement;其余信息(假期、纪念日、Jane)都是背景而非问题。

**干扰项迷惑性:**
- A:annual leave 是原文真词,但原文是 "I have annual leave to take"(有假),选项扭成"假没批"——事实否定。
- B:anniversary 是原文真词,但原文是 "the anniversary is looming" 且他主动筹备——方向相反。
- D:Jane 全程未表态;seg008 只说她恐高。把"恐高"偷换成"拒绝去 Barcelona"。

**为什么不能靠关键词:** 每个干扰项都是"原词 + 一处扭转";不跟踪事实的真假方向,四个选项看起来都"听过"。

**Provenance:** seg002(annual leave / anniversary / Barcelona 背景)、seg004(问题)、seg008(Jane 相关信息)。

### Check B2(initial_vs_final_decision / temporal_change)

**Question:** What is the man's accommodation plan by the end of the conversation?

- A) Book a glamorous hotel in the city for the whole week
- B) Spend a couple of nights somewhere glamorous, then the rest of the week further up the coast ✓
- C) Book an all-inclusive place inside the city of Barcelona
- D) Give up the trip because everything is fully booked

**正确答案需要理解的关系:** 方案的形成时序:初始意图(在 Barcelona 订)→ W 提出分段方案(seg005)→ M 接受并补充细节(seg006)。initial 与 final 的区分是核心考点。

**干扰项迷惑性:**
- A:glamorous / whole week 都是原文真词,但 "glamorous" 在最终方案里只占"a couple of nights";这是**初始方向**(全城订)与**最终方案**(分段)的混淆。
- C:all-inclusive 是原文真词,但原文明确这类地方在 **outside** the city(seg006)——地点范围偷换。
- D:fully booked 是(seg004)的**问题**,不是**结局**;把障碍当成结论。

**为什么不能靠关键词:** glamorous / all-inclusive / fully booked 全部真实出现;区分依赖"哪个方案最终被接受"与"每个修饰词修饰的是哪一段/哪里"。

**Provenance:** seg002(初始意图)、seg005(提议)、seg006(接受 + all-inclusive 位置)、seg004(fully booked 出处)。

### Check B3(attitude_target / speaker_swap)

**Question:** Why does the man decide against going up the cathedral tower?

- A) Because he is terrified of heights himself
- B) Because Jane is terrified of heights ✓
- C) Because the spiral staircase is still under construction
- D) Because the climb up would take too much time

**正确答案需要理解的关系:** 态度的归属目标——恐高的是 Jane 而不是 M(seg008 "Jane is terrified of heights");这决定了"不带她去登顶"的逻辑。

**干扰项迷惑性:**
- A:最典型的 speaker swap;"terrified of heights" 原文真词,主语偷换为 M 自己。
- C:缝合 seg003("cathedral still building after 150 years")与 seg007("steep spiral staircase")两个真实事实,推出一个对话中不存在的因果(楼梯在建所以不能上)。
- D:steep(陡)被替换成"耗时长"——邻近概念偷换;时间从未被提及。

**为什么不能靠关键词:** 所有选项的词(terrified of heights / staircase / building / climb)都在原文;必须精确回答"谁怕高"这个归属问题。

**Provenance:** seg003(教堂在建)、seg007(登顶+旋转楼梯)、seg008(Jane 恐高 + M 的决定)。
